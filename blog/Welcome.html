About FHS-BAP tutorials

November 22, 2024
Ting Fang Alvin Ang
Leader, FHS-BAP Data Core
Qiushan Tao
Co-leader, FHS-BAP Data Core

We’re delighted to have you join us. These tutorials are part of our ongoing efforts to empower researchers, clinicians, and data enthusiasts with the tools and knowledge needed to make the most of the rich datasets collected through the Framingham Heart Study Brain Aging Program (FHS-BAP).

🎯 Our Mission​

As the central hub for managing and sharing invaluable resources, the FHS-BAP Data Core is dedicated to advancing Alzheimer’s Disease (AD) research. We ensure the 📊 integrity, 🔍 accessibility, and 🛠️ usability of our data, fostering groundbreaking discoveries and promoting collaboration across disciplines.

💡 What to Expect Today​

  • 🔗 Explore our datasets—what they contain and how they’re structured.
  • 🖥️ Hands-on guidance for accessing and analyzing the data effectively.
  • 📋 Best practices for preserving the integrity of historical data while leveraging cutting-edge analytical techniques.
  • ❓ Opportunities to ask questions and engage with our team of experts.

Whether you’re a seasoned researcher or just beginning your journey into AD biomarkers, these tutorials are tailored to enhance your understanding FHS-BAP data and help you contribute to the advance researches related to neurodegenerative diseases.

Let’s work together to unlock the full potential of FHS-BAP data and pave the way for new breakthroughs in brain aging research. 🚀
